Privacy notice

Last updated 12 October 2026

Card Co-Pilot is still in development. This notice covers this website and its waitlist only. A full notice for the app will be published before launch.

What we collect on this website

  • Your email address, which is required to join the waitlist.
  • Your name, which is optional.

This website never asks for card numbers, bank logins or passwords.

Why we collect it

Only to send you launch updates and onboarding instructions for Card Co-Pilot. We do not use these details for anything else.

Who handles it

Your details are sent to the service that stores the waitlist. We will name that service here when the waitlist goes live.

Third parties on this website

  • The website is hosted by Vercel, which receives standard request logs when you visit.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • The website does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your details or stop emailing you at any time. Contact details will be published here before launch.

The app, planned

The app is designed so that two sources are optional and off until you choose them:

  • Gmail, with read-only access, to decode card statements. You can skip this during setup.
  • Bank transaction SMS, where your phone supports it, parsed on your phone with an option to confirm each one before it is saved.

The app notice will describe these in full, including consent and data controls. Details may change before launch.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page and its date when anything changes.
